# Financial Micro Structure & Exchange Intro, 2:ed
 
An introduction to how market microstrucure and exchanges work for people with no financial background.
Choose the format that suits you best. If you have access to an ebook reader I
recommend the **EPUB** format as that gives you freedom to choose font size and
background color. 

This format is compatible with both Apple "Book" reader and Adroid "PlayReader"

The **B5 format** is a standard large book format (but smaller than A4) 
and therefore gives a better view on tablet (which in general is smaller than A4)
